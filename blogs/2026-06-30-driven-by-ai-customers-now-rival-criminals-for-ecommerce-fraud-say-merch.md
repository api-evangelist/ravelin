Ravelin's Global Fraud Trends 2026 report finds ecommerce fraud losses up 7.5% year-over-year to an average of $11.4 million per merchant. Merchants now view customer-driven fraud as equally threatening to criminal fraud, with 43% reporting increased customer losses and two-thirds encountering AI-enabled fraud attacks.
